## 🧩 About

**Factorio Client Tool** is a comprehensive client-side utility for Factorio featuring factory management, resource tracking, crafting automation, map overlay, production statistics, and blueprint tools.

Based on popular mods like **Factorio Planner**, **Helmod**, and **Factory Planner**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏭 Factory Management
- Resource Tracker — monitor real-time resource production and consumption
- Production Statistics — detailed graphs and ratios for all items
- Factory Planner — calculate optimal production chains
- Blueprint Manager — import, export, and organize blueprints
- Automatic Crafting — queue and automate crafting tasks

### 🗺️ Map & Overlay
- Map Overlay — see resource patches, enemy bases, and terrain
- Radar Expansion — reveal map chunks and monitor biter activity
- Pathfinding Helper — optimize belt and train routes
- Chunk Explorer — highlight unexplored areas
- Pollution Viewer — visualize pollution spread and absorption

### 🛠️ Utility Tools
- Quick Bar Customization — extended hotbar with custom presets
- Inventory Manager — sort, filter, and auto-transfer items
- Train Scheduler — automate train routes and schedules
- Circuit Network Helper — debug and visualize circuit signals
- Mod Compatibility — works with most major mods
